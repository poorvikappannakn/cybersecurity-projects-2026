# SME Cybersecurity Risk Assessment Model

## Framework Approach

This project uses the NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF) 2.0 as the foundation for organizing cybersecurity risk management activities.

The project adapts the framework into a practical risk assessment process for small and medium enterprises (SMEs).

## Risk Assessment Process

1. Identify Assets
2. Identify Threats
3. Identify Vulnerabilities
4. Assess Likelihood
5. Assess Impact
6. Calculate Risk
7. Classify Risk
8. Map Risks to NIST CSF
9. Recommend Mitigation
10. Reassess Residual Risk
