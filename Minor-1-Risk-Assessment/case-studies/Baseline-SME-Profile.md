# Baseline SME Profile

## Business Overview

**Business Type:** Small Retail & E-commerce Company  
**Number of Employees:** 15

The organization operates a physical retail business and an online e-commerce platform.

## Critical Assets

1. Customer database
2. Employee email accounts
3. E-commerce website
4. Payment/POS system
5. Employee laptops
6. Business documents
7. Internal Wi-Fi/network
8. Cloud storage

## Baseline Security Posture

- Basic antivirus protection is installed.
- Password-based authentication is used for business accounts.
- Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is not consistently enabled.
- Security awareness training is informal.
- Software patching is performed, but there is no formal vulnerability-management process.
- Backups are maintained, but backup restoration is not regularly tested.
- The organization does not have a dedicated cybersecurity team.

## Purpose of the Baseline

This baseline profile provides a consistent fictional SME environment for applying the cybersecurity risk assessment framework.

The identified assets, threats, vulnerabilities, and existing controls will be used to assess inherent risk and later evaluate the effect of recommended security controls.
